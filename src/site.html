Music

An 1893 organ, played with affection

“Another 90s song that begs to be played on a church!”

The Hook & Hastings tracker organ was shipped from Boston in pieces and assembled in the space above the altar it was built for. An organ company called it the finest of its kind in New England.

It is maintained by our own organist, Stephan Conrady, who trained as an organ builder in Germany. It plays hymns beautifully. It also plays other things.

The choir sings from September to mid-June, and anyone who can carry a tune is welcome — some read music, some learn by ear.

Finding us

In the middle of St. Albans

Northwest Vermont, twenty-five miles up the lake from Burlington, facing Taylor Park. The clock in the tower has been keeping the town’s time since 1893.
